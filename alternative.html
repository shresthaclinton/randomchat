Looking for an Omegle or OmeTV alternative?

Online Galli offers free, random one-to-one video chat for adults aged 18 and over. If you want to meet someone new through a spontaneous conversation, you can start in your browser.

Built with Nepali users in mind, Online Galli combines live conversations with games, playful face accessories and positive reactions. You do not need to download a separate app.

Try Online Galli

What you can do on Online Galli

  • Meet a new person: join the matching queue for a one-to-one video conversation.
  • Break the ice: invite your partner to a game or send a friendly reaction.
  • Try playful face effects: add accessories such as glasses and hats without beauty filters that reshape your face.
  • Move to another conversation: use Next when you are ready to meet someone else.
  • Report or block: use the available controls when someone behaves inappropriately.

What to know before choosing it

Online Galli is in beta. Matching depends on another eligible person being available, so you may have to wait. A particular country or type of partner is not guaranteed. Camera and microphone access, a compatible browser and a working internet connection are needed for video chat.

The core video chat is free. Features and availability may change as the beta develops. Reporting and blocking help you respond to unwanted behavior, but they cannot guarantee that every conversation will be safe.

How to start

  1. Open the Online Galli homepage and read the rules.
  2. Confirm that you are 18 or older and agree to the terms.
  3. Choose “Enter the Galli” and allow camera and microphone access.
  4. Wait for a partner, then start a friendly conversation.

Is Online Galli connected to Omegle or OmeTV?

No. Online Galli is an independent service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Omegle or OmeTV. Their names are used here to help visitors understand the kind of alternative they are exploring. This page describes Online Galli; it does not claim feature parity with either service.

Protect your privacy while chatting

Keep your address, phone number, passwords and financial details private. Other participants may record or screenshot a call. Leave uncomfortable conversations and report rule violations. Do not record or share another participant’s image without their permission.
