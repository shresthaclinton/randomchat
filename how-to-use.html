How to Use Online Galli

Online Galli is a free random video chat that runs entirely in your browser. No app or account is required.

Step-by-step guide

  1. Open onlinegalli.com on your phone or computer.
  2. Confirm you are 18 or older and agree to the rules.
  3. Click Enter the Galli.
  4. Allow camera and microphone access when your browser asks.
  5. Wait a few seconds. You will be matched with another person who is also waiting.
  6. Talk, use face stickers, or play games together.
  7. Click Next when you want to meet someone new, or Exit to leave.

Tips

  • Use a stable internet connection for the best video quality.
  • Headphones reduce echo.
  • You can report or block anyone who makes you uncomfortable.
