What is Online Galli?

Online Galli is a free random video chat for adults. You are matched one-to-one with a stranger for a spontaneous conversation. There is no account to create and no app to install — it runs in your browser.

How random matching works

Click Enter the Galli. The system looks for another adult who is also waiting. When a match is found, your cameras connect and you can talk. Either person can leave at any time and be matched with someone new.

Games and face stickers

While you are connected you can invite the other person to multiplayer games such as Tic-Tac-Toe, Connect Four, Rock Paper Scissors, Would You Rather, and more. You can also turn on face stickers — glasses, hats, a moustache, and other accessories — that appear on your video for both of you. Some games and features are marked Pro.

Safety and privacy

Online Galli is for people 18 and older. Do not share your phone number, address, or other private details. Do not record the other person without permission. If someone makes you uncomfortable, use Report or Block. Read the Community Guidelines, Privacy Notice, and Terms before you start. Learn more on the About page.
