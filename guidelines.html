Online Galli is intended for adults (18+) who want spontaneous one-to-one conversations. Treat strangers with respect.

Not allowed

  • Anyone under 18 using the service, or content depicting or exploiting minors.
  • Nudity, sexual acts, sexual solicitation, or exposing someone to sexual content without consent.
  • Harassment, threats, hate speech, bullying, discrimination, or unwanted repeated contact.
  • Scams, spam, impersonation, fraud, or sharing malicious links.
  • Recording, photographing, publishing, or sharing another person's conversation or likeness without their informed consent.
  • Sharing someone else's private details or pressuring anyone to reveal personal information.
  • Illegal activities or encouraging harm.

Protect yourself

Do not share your phone number, address, passwords, financial information, or other sensitive details. Leave any uncomfortable conversation immediately.

Reporting

Use the Report or Block controls during a conversation. Reports may be reviewed by moderators and may lead to restrictions or bans. For urgent danger, contact local emergency services.
