Is Online Galli Safe?

Online Galli is designed for adults 18 and older. Like any random video chat, you should use it carefully.

Built-in safety features

  • Report button — flag users who break the rules
  • Block button — stop matching with the same person again
  • Automated nudity detection on video
  • No accounts required — less personal data stored

Safety tips

  • Never share your phone number, address, or social media.
  • Do not record or screenshot the other person without permission.
  • End the chat immediately if you feel uncomfortable.
  • Use the Report and Block tools.
