Enter Online Galli

How to start a conversation

  1. Open Online Galli and read the community rules. Confirm that you are at least 18 and agree to the terms.
  2. Choose “Enter the Galli” and allow camera and microphone access in your browser.
  3. Wait for another available participant. Once connected, you can see and hear each other.
  4. Use Next when you want to meet someone else. Report or block users who break the rules.

A little more fun in every conversation

Try playful face accessories, send positive reactions or invite your chat partner to a game. These features give you ways to break the ice while keeping the conversation friendly.

Frequently asked questions

Is Online Galli free?

Yes. The core one-to-one video chat is free to use.

Who is Online Galli for?

Online Galli is designed with Nepali adults in mind. You must be 18 or older. It does not guarantee a partner from a particular location.

Do I need to download an app?

No separate app is required. Open the website in a browser that supports camera and microphone access.

Why am I waiting for a partner?

You need another eligible participant to be available at the same time. Waiting can take longer when fewer people are online. Check camera and microphone permissions if you cannot start.

What should I keep private?

Do not share your address, phone number, passwords or financial details. Other participants may record or screenshot a call. Leave an uncomfortable conversation and use Report or Block when appropriate.
